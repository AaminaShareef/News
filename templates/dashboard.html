Risk Scoring Formula
💡 How Risk Scores Are Calculated
FORMULA
Risk Score = Severity × 0.40 + Article Volume × 0.25 + Source Diversity × 0.20 + Recency × 0.15
Severity weight Language signals (halt/crisis/collapse) + Source trust (Reuters=1.0 · AP=0.9 · BBC=0.85 · blog=0.3)
Recency boost Last 24 hrs ×2.0  ·  Last week ×1.0  ·  Older ×0.5
Risk thresholds ≥4.5 CRITICAL · ≥3.5 HIGH · ≥2.0 MEDIUM · <2.0 LOW
Sentiment weights 🔴 Negative ×1.00  ·  🟡 Neutral ×0.55  ·  🟢 Positive ×0.15
